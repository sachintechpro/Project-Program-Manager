OUTCOME SIGNALS

What we measure

  • Elapsed time from notice received to registered claim
  • First assignment / specialist activation time
  • Rework, exception rate and handoff count
  • Broker / insured acknowledgment timeliness
  • Adoption, quality and control exceptions after release
DECISION LOGIC

What we prioritize

  • Severity and business value of existing pain point
  • Frequency and volume of impacted claims
  • Dependencies on policy, vendor and claims data
  • Delivery capacity, readiness and compliance controls
  • Expected improvement with measurable evidence

LPM-ALIGNED GOVERNANCE

Change the decisions, not just the vocabulary

  1. Set guardrailsBudget, capacity, risk appetite and regulatory controls
  2. Prioritize outcomesClaims processing pain points, business value and dependencies
  3. Fund incrementallyGovern investments with periodic review rather than an unchecked fixed scope
  4. Inspect evidenceDeliveries, financial forecast, operational results and risks
  5. Adapt responsiblyEscalate material funding, scope and risk decisions to the rightful owner
06 / JOINING THE TEAM

First 90 days.
Useful from day one.

A practical, non-presumptive entry plan for an existing Agile environment with a change portfolio moving toward value-stream funding.

01–30
LISTEN + BASELINE

Understand the actual operating model

  • Meet sponsors, PMO, claims and tech leads
  • Review active commitments, funding and decision rights
  • Map claims process, key dependencies and delivery blockers
  • Validate baselines, outcomes, risks and reporting needs

Output: Agreed delivery baseline + top-five issue/dependency view

31–60
STABILIZE + ALIGN

Make the portfolio-to-team link visible

  • Build integrated initiative plan and financial forecast
  • Define risks, release criteria and escalation owners
  • Connect value-stream outcomes with existing Agile backlogs
  • Test a lean executive reporting rhythm

Output: One credible, sponsor-reviewed delivery dashboard

61–90
DELIVER + LEARN

Prove a better decision cycle

  • Support first usable increments / pilot as feasible
  • Review delivery data, exceptions and adoption
  • Recommend priority / capacity changes with evidence
  • Capture lessons for repeatable portfolio governance

Output: Benefits evidence + next-quarter delivery decisions
