05 / LEAN PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT & ADOPTION

Transform how we decide.
Keep how we deliver.

Most delivery teams may already use Agile while the change portfolio still manages fixed projects. This playbook focuses on the integration between the two, not prescribing a company-wide transformation from one PM role.

ILLUSTRATIVE CURRENT APPROACH

Waterfall-style change portfolio

Individual project proposals, fixed scope/budget commitments, annualized approval gates and reporting against original project milestones—even where execution teams run sprints.

ILLUSTRATIVE TARGET APPROACH

Value-stream-oriented portfolio

Strategic outcomes and bounded funding, capacity-aware initiative priority, incremental release evidence, quarterly investment review and benefits validation, with continued financial and risk governance.

PORTFOLIO ADOPTION PLAYBOOK

Practical transition controls

LEADERSHIP / PMO RESPONSIBILITIES

Strategy and authority

  • Define value streams and business outcome ownership
  • Approve funding guardrails, governance, prioritization approach
  • Establish transformation standards and portfolio review cadence
  • Resolve enterprise conflicts and authorize material changes
DELIVERY MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES

Make the operating model work

  • Link initiative scope and backlog to measurable outcomes
  • Manage multi-team dependencies, schedule and financial forecast
  • Provide decision-quality evidence and unblock teams
  • Coach better cross-team ways of working and escalate exceptions
FIRST 90 DAYS

Enter with evidence, not assumptions

01–30

Understand and baseline

Meet sponsors, PMO, claims/UW ops, tech and vendors. Validate value-stream definitions, existing Agile practices, business KPI baseline, actual commitments, funding and decision rights.

Output: Verified charter / portfolio view, top risks and dependency map.
31–60

Connect and stabilize

Build a shared integrated plan, benefits case, monthly forecast, meaningful leadership dashboard and cross-team blockers review. Align to current PMO standards.

Output: Sponsor-reviewed plan, RAG, EAC and decision log.
61–90

Deliver and adapt

Support an increment or pilot as feasible; inspect operational evidence, refine priority and capacity recommendations, mentor on handoffs and get approvals for any material changes.

Output: Delivery evidence, benefits plan and prioritized next-quarter decisions.
